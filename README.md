# VFabrica

**Global Textile Sourcing Platform**

VFabrica is a B2B marketplace that connects textile manufacturers, fabric mills, and wholesale buyers worldwide. The platform enables direct sourcing of fabrics, from organic cotton to technical textiles, through verified supplier profiles.

---

## Overview

VFabrica provides a digital marketplace where textile buyers can discover fabrics, compare specifications, and place orders directly with authenticated manufacturers. Suppliers can list their product catalogs, manage inventory, and fulfill orders through a unified platform.

The marketplace serves three primary roles:

- **Buyers** - Fabric sourcing professionals and procurement teams
- **Suppliers** - Textile mills, manufacturers, and wholesale distributors
- **Administrators** - Platform management and supplier verification

---

## Marketplace

The VFabrica marketplace features thousands of fabric listings across categories including cotton, silk, wool, linen, synthetic fibers, denim, technical fabrics, and embroidery materials. Every supplier undergoes a verification process to ensure authenticity and quality standards.
